THE BEAUTY OF THE PHILIPPINES

Puerto Princesa

Puetro Princesa—a highly urbanized coastal city on Palawan Island in the western Philippines and serves as the capital and eco-tourism hub of the province.

Chocolate Hills

Located in Bohol, the Chocolate Hills are a natural wonder consisting of hundreds of unique cone-shaped hills. They turn brown during the dry season.

Intramuros

Intramuros (Latin for "within the walls") is the historic core and oldest district of Manila, Philippines. Spanning 0.67 square kilometers along the southern bank of the Pasig River, it served as the seat of government and political, military, and religious center of the Spanish Empire in Asia for over three centuries.

Siargao

Siargao is a tear-drop-shaped island in Surigao del Norte, Philippines, widely known as the Surfing Capital of the Philippines.
